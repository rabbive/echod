ECHO Protocol vs Raft baseline

ECHO extends Raft with energy-aware vote gating — coordinators below Tlow = 15% battery become observer and leave the quorum, preventing depleted nodes from disrupting consensus. Once battery recovers past Trestore = 25% they re-enter as follower.

    Glossary — what am I seeing?

    Leader — coordinator that accepts replicated writes; only one per healthy cluster per term.

    Term — monotonic election generation; increments on each new election win.

    Max commit — highest commit index applied across all nodes.

    Leaves — registered mock sensors; their traffic appears as log entries.

    Observer — node below energy threshold, removed from voting quorum.

    Charts / table — live via MQTT → Socket.IO. If nothing moves, check status indicators in the header.
